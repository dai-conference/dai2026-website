Camera-Ready Guide

Instructions for accepted papers in the Research Track, Industry Track, and AI Paper Track.

Camera-Ready · 01

Submit on OpenReview

Please submit your camera-ready paper by 16 October 2026, 23:59 Anywhere on Earth (AoE, UTC-12).

Sign in to OpenReview, open your original accepted submission, and select Camera Ready Revision. Upload your final PDF, update the permitted submission fields as needed, and save the revision. Use the existing paper submission; do not create a new one.

Research Track on OpenReview Industry Track on OpenReview AI Paper Track on OpenReview

Before the deadline, verify that the uploaded PDF opens correctly and that the saved revision contains the intended final version.

Camera-Ready · 02

Template and Page Limits

Prepare your paper in English using the official ACM LaTeX template in the two-column sigconf format. Research and Industry Track papers use:

\documentclass[sigconf]{acmart}

AI Paper Track papers must also use the nonacm option and the non-archival template below.

The camera-ready allowance includes one additional page compared with the original submission limit:

TrackCamera-Ready Page Limit
Research TrackUp to 9 pages
Industry TrackUp to 7 pages
AI Paper TrackUp to 9 pages

For all three tracks, references and appendices do not count toward these limits. Optional appendices may be included after the references, with no page limit.

Remove the anonymous and review options. Include complete author names, affiliations, and acknowledgments where applicable. Preserve the template's prescribed fonts, font sizes, margins, column layout, and spacing.

No new authors may be added. The order of existing authors may be changed.

Camera-Ready · 03

Research and Industry Tracks: LaTeX Template

This section applies to the Research and Industry Tracks only. AI Paper Track authors should use the non-archival template below.

Download the complete Research / Industry LaTeX template (.tex)

In your ACM template project, replace the original main .tex file (for example, sample-sigconf.tex or main.tex) with this downloaded file. Rename it to the original main filename, or select it as the main document in Overleaf.

Copy your paper's title, author information, abstract, keywords, CCS concepts where applicable, body, acknowledgments, references, and optional appendices into the corresponding sections, replacing all example content. Keep acmart.cls, ACM-Reference-Format.bst, and your existing bibliography, figure, and section files in the project. The downloaded file includes instructions for using your own BibTeX bibliography.

This is a complete main document that compiles as supplied in the ACM template project. It already includes the DAI 2026 conference information and ACM Reference Format. The template automatically inserts your paper's author names, title, and page count. Unassigned publication details are left blank, and the ACM rights notice is temporarily suppressed.

This template is for the current OpenReview version only. Replace the temporary publication settings with the exact metadata and rights commands supplied by ACM when preparing the final publication submission.

Camera-Ready · 04

AI Paper Track: Non-Archival Format

AI Paper Track papers are non-archival and will not be included in the DAI 2026 ACM proceedings. The final paper will be retained on OpenReview as part of the track's public record. The ACM publication and APC instructions on this page apply to the Research and Industry Tracks only.

The 9-page limit, appendix policy, and author rules above also apply to this track. Follow the OpenReview submission instructions and final-paper checklist, together with the template below.

Complete LaTeX template

Download the complete AI Paper Track LaTeX template (.tex)

In your ACM template project, replace the original main .tex file (for example, sample-sigconf.tex or main.tex) with this downloaded file. Rename it to the original main filename, or select it as the main document in Overleaf.

Copy your paper's title, author information, abstract, keywords, CCS concepts where applicable, body, acknowledgments, references, and optional appendices into the corresponding sections, replacing all example content. Keep acmart.cls, ACM-Reference-Format.bst, and your existing bibliography, figure, and section files in the project. The downloaded file includes instructions for using your own BibTeX bibliography.

This is a complete main document that compiles as supplied in the ACM template project. The sigconf,nonacm class options and non-archival footer are already configured.

The template suppresses the ACM Reference Format block, copyright/licensing notice, DOI, and ISBN. Every page, including the first, displays the conference information, Non-archival paper, and the page number in the footer, without running headers.

Keep the paper's normal citations and References section. The ACM Reference Format block being removed is the publisher's suggested citation block near the beginning of the paper.

ARM Bundle and public materials

  • Update your ARM Bundle, including the AI Research Autonomy Disclosure, to reflect the final paper.
  • Follow the track's public-release requirements and document any necessary redactions.
  • Check that the paper title, abstract, and human-author information are accurate and consistent with the OpenReview record.
  • Verify that the uploaded PDF opens correctly, that the non-archival footer appears as intended, and that the saved revision contains the final paper and supporting materials.

Camera-Ready · 05

Final-Paper Checklist

  • Address the reviewers' feedback and any requirements stated in the decision or meta-review.
  • Check that the paper title, abstract, and author information are accurate and consistent between the PDF and OpenReview.
  • Update supplementary materials and disclosures where necessary, ensuring that they remain consistent with the final manuscript.
  • Confirm that the PDF uses the required format and page limit, includes author information, and contains no sample conference or publication values from the template.

At least one author of each accepted paper must register for the conference and present the work. For AI Paper Track papers, this must be a human author. Further instructions on registration and presentation arrangements will be sent separately.

Camera-Ready · 06

ACM Publication Process

For Research and Industry Track papers.

The camera-ready version submitted at this stage will be archived on OpenReview and used to initiate the ACM publication workflow, including the mandatory research integrity checks for ACM International Conference Proceedings Series (ICPS) proceedings.

Once these checks have been completed and the results cleared by ACM, we will follow up with authors about the final publication requirements, conference-specific publication metadata, and instructions for completing the ACM rights process.

For the current OpenReview version, use the conference information provided above and leave unassigned publication details blank. Replace the temporary settings with the official information when preparing the final submission to ACM. Please use the information provided at that stage when preparing the publication version, rather than the sample values included in the template.

Important Publication Information

ACM Open Access and Article Processing Charges

For Research and Industry Track papers. AI Paper Track papers are non-archival and are not part of the ACM proceedings.

Important note to authors about ACM's new open access publishing model

ACM has introduced a new open access publishing model for the International Conference Proceedings Series (ICPS). Authors based at institutions that are not yet part of the ACM Open program and do not qualify for a full geographic waiver will be required to pay an article processing charge (APC) to publish their ICPS article in the ACM Digital Library. To determine whether or not an APC will be applicable to your article, please follow the detailed ACM author guidance.

Any applicable APC is the responsibility of the authors.
