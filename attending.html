Venue & Hotels

DAI 2026 is hosted at City University of Hong Kong in Hong Kong.

Venue · CityU · Hong Kong Nearest MTR · Kowloon Tong 22.3364° N · 114.1733° E

Venue · 01

City University of Hong Kong

Sessions are held at the Yeung Kin Man Academic Building on the university campus, with workshops and posters in adjacent buildings.

City University of Hong Kong 83 Tat Chee Avenue Kowloon Hong Kong SAR
Nearest MTR Kowloon Tong Station connects to Festival Walk and the CityU campus by footbridge. Follow signs toward City University of Hong Kong from the mall concourse.
